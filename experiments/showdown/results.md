# Guardrail showdown results

`hybrid-<encoder>` = the encoder owns prompt injection and jailbreak (v4's `prompt_injection` and `jailbreak` questions dropped); v4 keeps every other risk.

Generated by `experiments/showdown/showdown.py report`. Input-stage sets are out of distribution for s1guard (never used in training) except `bench_test`. Latency is per case on an Apple M4 Pro (MPS), batch size 1, after warm-up. Each guard uses its own operating threshold: 0.5 for the encoders, the calibrated policy for s1guard, and for the ensembles s1-v4 OR the encoder at its 1%-FPR threshold on our benchmark dev split.
